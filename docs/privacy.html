The Hollow Below - Privacy policy

This policy describes the Microsoft Store edition of The Hollow Below, version 0.22.58. The publisher is Ryan VerWey. The game supports offline solo play and optional private online co-op with voice chat. It contains no advertising, in-app purchases or paid subscription.

OFFLINE PLAY AND LOCAL STORAGE
Solo play runs locally without joining an online session. Windows and Unity may store local display, audio and control preferences and technical diagnostic logs. The game also stores your chosen survivor/display name, voice preferences and a Unity anonymous-authentication identity/token locally for online services. This build has no saved campaign or run progress. Do not use a real name or other private information as your display name if you do not want other lobby members to see it.

PRIVATE ONLINE CO-OP
Choosing online co-op connects to Unity Gaming Services for anonymous authentication and Relay connectivity. A separate player-account signup is not required. Unity processes service identifiers, network/IP information, technical connection/device information and operational data needed to provide and protect its services. The host runs the gameplay server. The host and connected participants receive the session information needed to play, such as display names, survivor choices, readiness, positions, actions, game progress and results. Lobby invite codes allow people who possess the code to attempt to join; share them only with intended participants. Online co-op requires Internet access and compatible game versions.

OPTIONAL MICROPHONE AND VOICE CHAT
Voice supports Off, push to talk and open-microphone modes. Microphone capture requires the applicable Windows microphone permission. A local microphone test plays locally and does not transmit. The game does not send microphone audio before joining a lobby. During a joined session, enabled voice is encoded and sent through the host/Unity Relay game connection to the intended team or proximity audience. The host processes decoded audio, including voice loudness used by the game's hunter mechanics. The game does not save voice recordings or provide a developer-operated voice-recording service. Other participants hear transmitted speech and may independently record what they hear; the game cannot prevent this. Avoid speaking personal information you do not want participants to receive. Voice settings provide microphone selection, Off/push-to-talk/open-mic controls, local mute and individual participant muting. Transmission pauses while configuring voice or when the game loses focus.

PROVIDERS AND DIAGNOSTICS
Unity's services process information under Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy . Service providers may retain operational/security records according to their policies. Windows and Microsoft process Store installation, updates, permissions and Store diagnostics under Microsoft's privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement . Unity legacy player analytics and cloud crash uploading are disabled for this release; this does not mean online service providers receive no connection or operational data.

SUPPORT AND YOUR CHOICES
You can play solo without joining a lobby, leave an online session, turn voice Off, mute participants, and manage microphone permission in Windows Settings. Local preferences and anonymous service credentials can be removed by resetting/removing the app's local data, which also clears local settings. This game does not sell personal information or use advertising profiles.

Support and privacy requests: https://github.com/RyanVerWey/the-hollow-below-alpha/issues . This is a public issue tracker: do not post passwords, authentication tokens, full logs, private contact details or voice recordings. If a request needs private information, ask for an appropriate private contact route before sharing it. GitHub processes public issue reports under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . We will update this policy before distributing material changes in these practices.
