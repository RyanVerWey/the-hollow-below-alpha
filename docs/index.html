What is in this alpha?

A single-player maze through the pumping station, three breakers to restore, a stalking hunter, hiding lockers, a rechargeable flashlight, and a new exploration and pursuit soundscape.

How do updates work?

Choose Check for updates in the launcher. When a new alpha is available, download its full installer and run it again. The launcher can verify your game files; re-running the installer restores any damaged files. Installed play works offline.

Can I play on my phone or with a controller?

This alpha is built for a desktop keyboard and mouse. Mobile, controller support, multiplayer, and saved progress are not available.

Prefer a portable copy?

Download the full Windows ZIP, extract it completely, then open The Hollow Below.exe. The installer is recommended for shortcuts and the launcher.

Is this on Steam?

Not yet. This is an early independent playtest, shared directly while the game grows.

Found something broken?

Send the person who shared the game your build number, Windows version, what happened, and the steps that caused it. A screenshot helps. For game logs, look in %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Blackwater Independent\The Hollow Below.
