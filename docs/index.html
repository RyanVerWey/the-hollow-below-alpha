What is in this alpha?

Godot alpha 0.23.12. Hunters now recognize exposed survivors across a longer visible corridor, including during the opening grace period. Safe rooms, real wall cover and uncompromised hiding remain protected. Deliberate visible approaches receive a short warning before pursuit movement. Everyone in a co-op group should update. Play solo or host private co-op for 2–4 players using invite codes. Choose from four survivors and save your own display name. The host selects either level and difficulty. Survivors start separately, share objectives and map discoveries, and must each escape. Death or escape lets you spectate. The host must stay connected; online menus do not pause the hunt. Internet is required for co-op; solo play remains offline. Two playable nightmares: Blackwater pumping station and Briarwake Estate, a moonlit hedge maze. Briarwake has a blood-moon sky, drifting haze, damp dirt paths and joined hedges. Its farmer wears a stitched hood and leather apron, carries a two-handed chainsaw, and uses authored walk, run and three attack animations. New iron oil lanterns mark corridor turns. Its chainsaw attacks have a visible windup, a committed swing and recovery; cover and retreat can prevent a hit. Briarwake has a safe potting house, dim lanterns, cold haze, tool shelters and the Thornkeeper: a demonic farmer carrying a chainsaw. Restore three wards in any order. The Lantern Ward strengthens guide lights and doubles portable-lantern endurance; the Wind Bell adds player tracking and a reusable nearby stagger; the Root Altar opens three thorn shortcuts. Any ward unlocks the M-toggle survey; seen objectives are recorded on the map; unseen locations remain hidden. Restore all three and return to the estate gate. Hear the saw approach before you see him. Both levels share the same HUD, map legend, objective checklist, pause controls and settings. Found objectives gain amber map pins; completed objectives turn green. Both levels offer Easy, Normal, Hard and Nightmare, with different routes, lighting, equipment and hunting. Nightmare removes portable light and sprint and locks most hiding places. Blackwater retains its breaker progression and live field map. Electrical buzzing grows louder near unpowered breakers, carries around corners and stops when restored. In Briarwake, follow lantern wind and loose metal, a creaking bell hanger with dull clapper, or dry root groans to dormant wards. Each falls silent when restored. Its harmless corner scares now have roughly half the former cooldown, with shorter retries when placement fails. Corn Maze and Stone Labyrinth remain Coming Soon. TAB shows objectives; Escape pauses and resumes. Restarting or leaving a run requires confirmation that progress will not be saved. Controls & settings offers Fullscreen, Borderless and Windowed, resolution, three graphics presets, V-Sync and a frame limit. Display changes revert unless confirmed. The title menu includes Quit to desktop.

How do I play with friends?

Open Play with Friends, enter your name and host a private lobby. Share its invite code with up to three friends. Everyone chooses a survivor and readies up; the host chooses the level and difficulty and starts the descent. You begin in different maze sectors, share objective progress and each need to reach the exit. No account or port forwarding is needed. Co-op requires Internet; the host must keep the game open. Menus do not pause online play. Solo play remains available offline.

How do updates work?

The launcher checks for updates when it opens. When a newer alpha is available, choose Update & restart. It downloads and verifies the full installer, installs the update, and reopens the launcher. Close the game before updating. Installed play stays available offline. Older launchers need the latest full installer once to enable this feature.

Can I play on my phone or with a controller?

This alpha is built for a desktop keyboard and mouse. Mobile, controller support and saved progress are not available.

Is this on Steam?

Not yet. This is an early independent playtest, shared directly while the game grows.

Found something broken?

Send the person who shared the game your build number, Windows version, what happened, and the steps that caused it. A screenshot helps. Windows logs: %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\The Hollow Below\logs.
