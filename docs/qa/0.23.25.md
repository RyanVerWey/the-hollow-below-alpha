# 0.23.25 — authored motion and hunter navigation

All four survivors now use Blender Auto-Rig Pro motion exports with 18 clips each: locomotion, directional/crouched movement, equipment carrying, and hiding transitions. Distance-driven playback and directional blending replace the previous stacked procedural gait. Equipment sockets and actual shoe contact were checked in Godot.

Repeater, Reaver and Thornkeeper use authored locomotion with contact-driven footsteps. Their distinct recordings are preserved. Attacks retain moving lower-body pursuit. The Widow retains its specialized eight-leg floor/wall solver.

All four hunter types use Godot NavigationRegion3D/NavigationAgent3D A* floor routes. Rotation is handled explicitly by the movement controller. Reaver has a wider navigation clearance and uses side passages to yield to opposing traffic; narrow corridors retain collision-checked passing behavior.
