The Hollow Below alpha 0.22.45 for Windows 10/11, 64-bit.

Close the game, open the launcher and choose Check for updates, then Update & restart. New players can use the full Windows installer below.

- Updated Windows alpha with Blackwater and Briarwake Estate. Corn Maze and Stone Labyrinth remain Coming Soon.
- Host departure now clears the ended session and loads a fresh main menu. Normal host closure and forced host termination were tested with four native peers in both levels, with additional internet Relay disconnect checks.
- Multiplayer maps now show small player color markers. The left-hand player legend holds names and matching colors; your row says YOU and your marker has a light outline. Live tracking still unlocks through objectives, and dead or escaped survivors no longer appear as active markers.
- Co-op results show time below, objective discovery and activation times, and each survivor outcome. Parties retain their invite code, survivor identities and lobby between rounds until the host leaves.
- Both levels use shared HUD and map layouts. Menus, long names, objective notes, results and settings have improved spacing, scrolling and text sizing. Native UI checks covered widescreen, ultrawide and 4:3 at enlarged HUD scale.
- Updated Blender survivor models and animation, original environment props and monster rigs. Briarwake has dirt paths, detailed foliage, trees, roots, haze, a blood-moon sky and darker ambient lighting so the handheld lantern helps.
- Difficulty scales maze sizes and objective placements are randomized. Objectives aid navigation and the final escape increases hunter pressure. Sight, footsteps, voices, hiding and broken contact remain part of detection.
- Co-op hunt warnings are personal to the targeted survivor. Map tracking reveals connected survivors only after the tracking objective is restored.
- Push-to-talk and open-mic crew voice use the existing host connection. Fullscreen, borderless and windowed modes, adjustable resolution, graphics presets and sound mixing are available in settings.
- Windows only. The standard installer includes the game and launcher. Close the game, then choose Update & restart in the launcher. Early alpha, keyboard and mouse, no saved run progress.

Validation: native UI captures in both levels at three aspect ratios and enlarged HUD; four actual peers through two rounds with tracking lock/unlock, live movement and inactive-player removal; eight local host-departure cases plus two internet Relay departures on the unchanged disconnect implementation.
