# The Hollow Below / Alpha 0.22.63

Blackwater and Briarwake now separate outer sound detection from the inner hunt range. Distant audible movement or speech draws the monster toward its last heard location to find line of sight; it does not grant an immediate full-speed chase.

Movement sound receives a stronger carry bonus as difficulty rises: Easy +25%, Normal +35%, Hard +45%, Nightmare +55%. Actual audibility still depends on gait, surface and corridor path distance. Speech detection receives a 10% range increase.

The host scales awareness by living players who have not escaped, including hidden survivors. Each extra player adds 6% hunt range and 10% detection range, capped at four players: +18% and +30%. Monster speed and attack reach do not increase with party size.

Sustained fresh sound inside the hunt range can commit a chase; outer sound cannot precharge that reaction. On Hard and Nightmare, sustained audible speech can also trigger a close-range sound hunt. Quiet relocation leaves only a finite last-heard clue, and competing distant speakers do not steal an established chase.

Opening grace, objective-response warnings, recovery windows, attack windups, protected unseen hiding and Nightmare walking-speed tuning are retained. Briarwake now explicitly guards its opening grace against both sight and sound acquisition.

After all objectives, maps distinguish amber outer sound limits from red inner hunt limits. These are awareness ceilings; a quiet footstep is not audible throughout the amber area.

Includes the Briarwake joined hedges, tucked ward sites, all-side ward interaction, garden potting shed and lore from 0.22.62. Windows download only. Close the game and choose Update & restart; all lobby players should use the same version.
