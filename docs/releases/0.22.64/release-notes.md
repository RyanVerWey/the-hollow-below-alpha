# The Hollow Below / Alpha 0.22.64

Blackwater solo hunts now finish the approach to a visible survivor at their actual position. The hunter no longer stops at the centre of a maze cell just outside reach when a player stands still near a wall or corner.

The final approach sweeps a collision capsule against solid walls and props, at the existing chase speed. Capture still requires visibility and the same one-metre reach; unseen or sound-only clues do not grant exact-position tracking.

Unseen lockers, the protected starting room, objective arrival warnings and opening grace remain protected. Cooperative pursuit and attack tuning are unchanged. The two-range sound detection and difficulty/party scaling from 0.22.63 are included.

Windows download only. Close the game and choose Update & restart in the launcher. All players in a lobby should use the same version.
